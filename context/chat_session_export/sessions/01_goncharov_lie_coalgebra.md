# Goncharov Lie Coalgebra as Physical Representation

The Goncharov Lie coalgebra of a field as a physical representation

The physical intuition is strong because perturbative QFT amplitudes often evaluate to **polylogarithms, multiple zeta values, and periods**. Goncharov’s framework gives these quantities a hidden algebraic skeleton: not just numbers, but motivic objects with coproducts/cobrackets.
