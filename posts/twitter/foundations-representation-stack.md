1/4
Does math describe physics, or get realized from it? Part I of the modular 7-paper series builds an explicit pipeline, Obs(M) = Obs(Real(Φ(M))), with a confidence label on every step.

2/4
Core unit is a "representation entry": (math object, physical rep, translation rule, epistemic status). The status ∈ {Standard, Heuristic, Speculative} forms an ordered algebra, not just a disclaimer.

3/4
We prove the pipeline is functorial, give an exact criterion for when local data glues into a global "representation stack," and show epistemic confidence can only decay as translations compose, never improve.

4/4
Part I of 7. Modular, not unified. Peer-reviewed, code-reviewed, Haskell+Lean verified.
